## About me

Obsessed with quantitative finance. Trading crypto on a personal portfolio since 2017, with 3+ years of experience spanning systematic trading of energy and commodity derivatives on global exchanges. Currently working as a Data Scientist on BI development, analytics and MLOps within the Australian Public Service, while developing data engineering skills to build end-to-end pipelines.

Hyper-obsessed with automation and leveraging AI to automate work. Motivated to apply my expertise at the intersection of systematic trading, data science and quantitative finance to generate alpha.

This GitHub is a combination of systematic trading strategies I'm publicly backtesting and personal data science, analytics and engineering projects I'm working on. Feel free to reach out to collaborate.
